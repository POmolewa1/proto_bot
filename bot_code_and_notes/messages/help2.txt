`/toggle_steam_sync`
If you have a Steam account linked, it will automatically be synced once per day at 12:00 AM.

If your Steam profile is private and you aren't friends with the bot owner's Steam account, automatic syncing will be turned off.

If syncing is turned off and you want to turn it back on, use this command.

You can check whether auto-sync is enabled by using `/guildcard` on your own profile and pressing **Next** until you reach your Steam page. At the bottom of the Steam page, you'll see the last time your account was synced and whether auto-sync is currently enabled.

🎮 **ACTIVITY & XP**

Your activity is tracked automatically in the background.

Playing games, spending time in voice calls, streaming, and other activities can contribute to your XP.

You don't need to manually start or stop anything.

I can track your games when your Discord activity is visible. If you've ever seen someone with **Playing: [game]** underneath their name, that's the information I look for.

If you have Steam linked, I can also record your Steam playtime when your Steam profile is public or when you are Steam friends with the bot owner.

🏆 **WEEKLY STATS & MVP**

Weekly statistics keep track of things such as:

• Games played
• Game time
• Voice activity
• Streaming
• Messages

At the end of each week, an MVP is selected based on their activity.

**MVP Points:**

• 6 points / hour of streaming
• 3 points / hour in voice calls
• 2 points / hour of gaming

XP uses a similar point system.
