📖 **Lysithea Help**

Here are some of the things I can do!

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👤 **PROFILE & ACCOUNT**

`/guildcard`
View your profile, level, XP, and games.
You can also view another guild member's card.

`/link_steam`
Link your Steam account so I can keep track of your games.

**Is it safe?**
Yes! Your Steam ID is public information, and giving it to Lysithea does **not** give me access to your Steam account. I cannot log into your account, see your password, or make changes to your account.

**How does it work?**

Every Steam profile has a public Steam ID. You can find it in the URL of a Steam profile.

For example:

`https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx/`

The `xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx` portion is the Steam ID.

Simply use:

`/link_steam xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx`

Lysithea will then ask you to confirm that the profile is yours before linking it.

**🔒 What needs to be public?**

If you are **not Steam friends with the bot owner (MegaJoltz)**, your Steam profile needs to be set to public for Lysithea to retrieve your games.

This is because Lysithea looks up your Steam profile and reads the game information that is publicly visible on it.

If you **are** Steam friends with the bot owner (MegaJoltz), Lysithea can access the information that the bot owner can see on your profile.

**🎮 What about games I don't want people to see?**

Don't worry!

Lysithea **cannot see games that are hidden by Steam's privacy settings**. It can only access the game information that is visible to the bot owner's Steam account.

Lysithea also only checks your Steam profile **once per day at 12:00 AM**. It does not constantly monitor your Steam account.

`/unlink_steam`
Unlink your Steam account and remove your stored Steam account information.

Any games Lysithea has already recorded from your activity will **remain in the server's records**, so unlinking your Steam account will not erase your existing game history.
